# Scoring Rubric

> For each major architecture question, use a **0–4 evidence-based rubric**.

---

## Rubric

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **0** | Doesn't understand the concept |
| **1** | Knows terminology |
| **2** | Can explain the mechanism |
| **3** | Can apply it to the scenario and identify trade-offs |
| **4** | Can reason through failure modes, alternatives, operational implications, and justify the final choice |

---

## Guidance for Interviewers

For an Architect role, put **less weight** on memorizing annotations/APIs and **much more weight** on the candidate's ability to say:

> "I would choose X instead of Y because..."

...then defend that choice when requirements or failure conditions change.

A score of **3 or 4** on the architecture and distributed systems questions is the bar for a strong architect hire.
